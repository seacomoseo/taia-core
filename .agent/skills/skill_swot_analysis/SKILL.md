# SWOT Analysis Skill

Perform internal and external strategic analysis to identify competitive advantages and risks.

## When to Use

- After Project Intake and Competitive Analysis.
- When defining brand positioning.
- Before crafting the Marketing Strategy.
- To identify USP (Unique Selling Proposition).

## Inputs

1. **Brief Summary**: From `skill_project_intake`.
2. **Competitive Analysis**: From `skill_competitive_analysis`.
3. **Market Trends**: General industry knowledge or research.

## Process

### Step 1: Internal Analysis

Identify what the business controls:
- **Strengths**: What do they do exceptionally well? (e.g., years of experience, unique tech, great reviews).
- **Weaknesses**: Where do they struggle? (e.g., small team, low visibility, outdated site).

### Step 2: External Analysis

Identify what the market dictates:
- **Opportunities**: Trends they can exploit. (e.g., rising demand in a neighborhood, competitors ignoring a segment).
- **Threats**: External risks. (e.g., big competitor entering market, changing regulations).

### Step 3: Strategic Matrix (DAFO)

Create the matrix in `/_context/strategy/swot-analysis.md`:

```markdown
# SWOT Analysis (DAFO)

## 🎯 Strengths (Fortalezas)
1. [Strength 1]
2. [Strength 2]
3. [Strength 3]

## ⚡ Weaknesses (Debilidades)
1. [Weakness 1]
2. [Weakness 2]
3. [Weakness 3]

## 🚀 Opportunities (Oportunidades)
1. [Opportunity 1]
2. [Opportunity 2]
3. [Opportunity 3]

## ⚠️ Threats (Amenazas)
1. [Threat 1]
2. [Threat 2]
3. [Threat 3]
```

### Step 4: Strategic Recommendations

Cross-reference findings:
- **Maximizing Strengths**: How to use them for Opportunities.
- **Mitigating Weaknesses**: How to fix them to avoid Threats.
- **USP Identification**: Define the "Unique Selling Proposition" based on Strengths vs Competitor Weaknesses.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/strategy/swot-analysis.md` - Full DAFO matrix and strategic insights.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Internal analysis covers at least 3 items per section.
- [ ] External analysis reflects findings from competitor research.
- [ ] Strategic recommendations are actionable for the website design.
- [ ] USP is clearly defined.
