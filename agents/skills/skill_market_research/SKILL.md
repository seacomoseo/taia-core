# Market Research

Turn broad market assumptions into concrete opportunity hypotheses.

## When to Use

- After intake, before committing to growth strategy.
- When the client lacks market clarity.

## Inputs

1. Business description and offer.
2. Geography and language scope.

## Process

1. Define market segments and TAM/SAM/SOM assumptions.
2. Identify major demand patterns and seasonality.
3. Identify competitor clusters and substitution behavior.
4. Produce ranked opportunities by impact and feasibility.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/market/market-landscape.md`
2. `/_context/market/segment-opportunities.md`
3. `/_context/market/risk-assumptions.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Segments are prioritized with rationale.
- [ ] Top opportunities are ranked and actionable.
- [ ] Risks and assumptions are explicit.
