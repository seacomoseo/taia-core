# Visibility Strategy

Design a realistic channel strategy aligned to business goals and resources.

## When to Use

- Marketing direction is unclear.
- Client needs a practical growth roadmap beyond the website.

## Inputs

1. Market, competitor, and persona outputs.
2. Current channels and resources.

## Process

1. Define goals and KPIs by funnel stage.
2. Select channels (SEO, SEM, social, email, partnerships, local).
3. Define channel roles, cadence, and dependencies.
4. Build 30/60/90-day plan.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/marketing/visibility-strategy.md`
2. `/_context/marketing/channel-plan.md`
3. `/_context/marketing/90-day-execution-plan.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] KPIs and channel roles are defined.
- [ ] Cadence and responsibilities are actionable.
- [ ] Plan is phased and realistic.
