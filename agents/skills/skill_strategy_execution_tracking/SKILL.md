# Strategy Execution Tracking

Convert plans into accountable execution loops.

## When to Use

- After strategy delivery.
- During ongoing implementation and follow-up.

## Inputs

1. Strategy outputs (SEO/SEM/visibility/content).
2. Team capacity and timeline constraints.

## Process

1. Break strategy into milestones and tasks.
2. Assign owner, due date, KPI, and dependency for each task.
3. Define weekly review cadence and status categories.
4. Build decision log for blockers and pivots.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/ops/execution-roadmap.md`
2. `/_context/ops/weekly-tracker.md`
3. `/_context/ops/decision-log.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Every strategic objective has executable tasks.
- [ ] KPIs and owners are assigned.
- [ ] Weekly review process is documented.
