# Buyer Persona

Build practical personas used by SEO, SEM, copy, and design decisions.

## When to Use

- Discovery and strategy phase.
- Messaging or offer positioning feels generic.

## Inputs

1. Intake and market context.
2. Existing client/customer notes.

## Process

1. Define ICP (firmographic/demographic fit).
2. Define 1-3 buyer personas with goals, pains, constraints, objections, and trust triggers.
3. Map awareness stages and likely search behavior.
4. Extract messaging principles per persona.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/strategy/icp.md`
2. `/_context/strategy/buyer-personas.md`
3. `/_context/strategy/persona-messaging-map.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Personas are specific and behavior-based.
- [ ] Objections and trust triggers are explicit.
- [ ] Messaging map can be consumed by copy and ads skills.
