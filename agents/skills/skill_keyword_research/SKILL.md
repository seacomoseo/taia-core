# Keyword Research

Translate business goals into an intent-first keyword plan.

## When to Use

- Before defining website structure or content plan.
- Before launching paid search campaigns.

## Inputs

1. Personas, offers, and market scope.
2. Current site and competitor context.

## Process

1. Build seed keyword universe by problem, solution, and intent.
2. Cluster keywords by search intent and journey stage.
3. Map clusters to page types (transactional, commercial, informational).
4. Prioritize clusters by business value and difficulty.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/seo/keyword-universe.md`
2. `/_context/seo/keyword-clusters.md`
3. `/_context/seo/keyword-to-page-map.md`
4. `/_context/sem/keyword-ad-groups.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Intent-based clustering is complete.
- [ ] Prioritized map exists for SEO pages and SEM groups.
- [ ] Cannibalization risks are documented.
