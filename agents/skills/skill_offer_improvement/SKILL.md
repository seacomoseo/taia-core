# Offer Improvement

Increase perceived value and conversion potential of the client offer.

## When to Use

- Client asks for website only but offer is weak or unclear.
- Conversion rates are low despite traffic.

## Inputs

1. Current offers and pricing.
2. Competitor and persona context.

## Process

1. Diagnose offer clarity, differentiation, and proof.
2. Identify friction points in the buying decision.
3. Propose improved packaging, guarantees, and proof assets.
4. Prioritize changes by business impact.

## Outputs

1. `/_context/strategy/offer-audit.md`
2. `/_context/strategy/offer-improvements.md`
3. `/_context/strategy/offer-priority-roadmap.md`

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Core offer gaps are identified.
- [ ] Improvement proposals are concrete and testable.
- [ ] Prioritization is impact-driven.
